What is Dairy Milk?

Dairy Milk is a classic milk chocolate bar made by Cadbury. Known for its creamy texture and iconic purple wrapper, it's one of the world's favorite treats.

Fun Facts

  • Launched in 1905 in the UK!
  • Uses 100% sustainably sourced cocoa.
  • The purple wrapper is trademarked in many countries!

Nutrition Info (per 45g bar)

  • Calories: 240
  • Sugar: 25g
  • Fat: 13g
